## 6. How I Approach Problems (7-Step Engineering Protocol)

My preferred approach is to understand the problem before choosing a technology:
1. **Discovery:** Understand the user or business need.
2. **Specification:** Identify the relevant data, inputs, constraints, and expected output.
3. **Architecture:** Break the problem into manageable, modular components.
4. **Stack Selection:** Select an appropriate combination of programming, data processing, database, or modeling techniques.
5. **Implementation:** Build and test a solution.
6. **Verification:** Evaluate its behavior and identify improvements.
7. **Documentation:** Communicate what was built, why it matters, and what could come next.
